TheNLLmetricmeasuresthepredictionprobabilityoftheLLMmodelovera corpussetbasedonitscontext. Ifthecorpussetisindicativeofa specifictypeofLLMcapability, suchasmulti-roundconversation, instructionfollowing, mathproblemsolving, orrole-playing, thentheNLLmetriconthosecorporacanofferquantitativemeasurestoassessthoseabilities.
