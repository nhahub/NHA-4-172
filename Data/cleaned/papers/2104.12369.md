Op-levelmodelparallelismreducesthememoryconsumptionbyslicingtheparametersandtheactivationmemory, however, itintroducescommunicationstokeepthedistributedtensorlayoutsconsistentbetweensuccessiveoperators. Thememorybenefitcomesfromthateachdeviceholdsa subsetoftotallayersofthemodel, andthecommunicationsonlyhappenattheboundariesofstages.
