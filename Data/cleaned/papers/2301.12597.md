### 2.1. End-To-EndVision-LanguagePre-TrainingVision-languagepre-trainingaimstolearnmultimodalfoundationmodelswithimprovedperformanceonvariousvisionand-languagetasks. WealigntheoutputqueryrepresentationZ fromtheimagetransformerwiththetextrepresentationt fromthetexttransformer, wheret istheoutputembeddingofthe [CLS] token.
